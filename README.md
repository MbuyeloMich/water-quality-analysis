# Water Quality Analysis Project

## 🎯 Discovery: Opposite Chemical Behaviors in Adjacent Catchments

Analysis of water quality data from uMhlathuze and LUVUVU catchments in South Africa revealed **fundamentally different chemical behaviors** despite similar geology.

### 🔬 Key Findings:
1. **Only 22.6% of LUVUVU water** meets WHO pH safety standards
2. **Opposite pH-chemical correlations**:
   - MHLATHUZE: Negative correlations (pH ↑ = chemicals ↓)
   - LUVUVU: Positive correlations (pH ↑ = chemicals ↑)
3. **Caused by**: Different pollution loads + water mixing patterns
